§ 01

A handover is a moment. A firm is a moving thing.

Most software is handed over once, to one person, on one afternoon. Then the firm changes and the configuration does not.

1.

The person trained at launch is often not the person administering it a year later. The knowledge leaves with them unless somebody is still available to ask.

2.

Firms hire, add practice areas, open offices, and change how consultations are routed. Every one of those is a configuration change somebody has to know how to make.

3.

New capabilities are worth nothing if nobody knows they arrived. A release note is not adoption.

§ 05

Common questions

Is onboarding included?

Yes. Every platform subscription includes configuration and walkthrough sessions for the people who will use GuideHerd.

Do we have to subscribe to Success?

No. The platform stands on its own. Most firms keep Success because configuration questions keep arriving — but it is a separate decision, and you can stop it without losing the platform.

Can you train new hires later?

Yes — that is one of the main reasons firms keep the subscription. New-staff onboarding is included in Success rather than quoted each time.

Will you make configuration changes for us?

We will walk your administrator through them, and on the Managed Configuration plan we will sit down monthly and do the work alongside them. Your firm always holds the administrative access.

Do you work with our IT provider?

Yes, and we prefer to. Microsoft 365 connections are set up with whoever administers your tenant, not around them.

Do you offer on-site sessions?

Yes, by quote. Remote is the default because it is faster and cheaper for the firm. On-site makes sense for a full-firm rollout.
