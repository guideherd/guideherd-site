# GuideHerd weekly fresh-media standard

Owner-approved direction: fresh, distinct visual assets every week, inspired by Notion's approachable editorial social strategy without copying its identity.

## Current assets
Four original SVG designs in this folder. These are **not** PNGs; the existing root `images/social/manifest.csv` catalogs 1080x1080 PNGs. Do not silently insert SVG entries into a PNG-only consumer. Render and validate 1080x1080 PNG derivatives first, then add the derivatives to the existing approved media inventory using its actual ingestion rules. Until conversion and ingestion are verified, these assets are staged, not Blotato-ready.

## Weekly unattended operating requirement
- Each week, plan three editorial themes but produce a target of 12–14 distinct platform-specific visual assets (Instagram 5–7 including carousel panels, Facebook 3, Threads 2, X 2). These are creative assets, not 12–14 paid generations. Adapt storytelling by platform; never simply crop or repost the same art across all four accounts.
- Treat the existing Blotato plan, weekly generation credit ceiling (previously discussed: 550 credits/week), and existing image-generation allowance as hard limits, not spending targets. Check remaining credits before every paid generation; never purchase credits or upgrade plans automatically. If a cap or remaining balance is uncertain, avoid paid generation until verified.
- Prefer original programmatic SVG/typography, existing approved unused media, and privacy-safe real product visuals to conserve credits. Threads and X may use text-only posts when appropriate. Do not generate images merely to hit the numeric target.
- Use the existing social agent, generation budget, GitHub repository and Blotato pipeline. No new infrastructure or purchases.
- Check actual supported product claims against the public GuideHerd site; avoid unsupported claims, fake screenshots, client data, customer names, and imitation of Notion trademarks or illustrations.
- Check spelling, visual quality, dimensions, contrast, licensing and mobile legibility. Reject malformed images.
- Deduplicate against recent posts and media inventory. Do not reuse one identical image across all four platforms in a day unless there is an explicit campaign reason.
- Publish only under existing social publishing permissions and approved playbook. Keep current schedule, credit ceilings and platform constraints; don't interpret this document as permission to expand posting authority.
- Log assets, source, date, usage, credits, platform posts, and live verification evidence. If no browser is available, report publication as unverified on live pages.
- When weekly creation or upload fails, fall back to approved unused library images; record the deficit and retry through existing authorized automation. Never fabricate an upload or successful publication.
