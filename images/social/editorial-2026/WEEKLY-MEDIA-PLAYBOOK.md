# GuideHerd social image-partner operating rules
Source: Claude's "GuideHerd Social: Brief for Image Partner" dated 2026-10-27. Publishing is operated by Claude through Blotato; ChatGPT supplies creative assets in guideherd-site.

## Actual live workflow
- Monday, Wednesday, Friday about 8:00 AM Central, Facebook, Instagram, Threads and X.
- Monday office problem; Wednesday how we build it or shipped; Friday build lesson or lighter note; holidays override.
- X image Monday/Friday, text-only Wednesday. Threads image most days. Instagram image every day, with one 2–3 panel carousel weekly. Facebook one image per post.
- Claude writes unique copy for each platform, checks product claims against guideherd.ai, verifies Blotato publication, and logs credits, images, IDs and analytics. Do not bypass its anti-duplicate checks.
- ChatGPT-supplied assets are staged until Claude updates the live playbook to prefer them. Blotato image generation remains fallback.

## Non-negotiable image requirements
- 1080x1080 PNG or JPG, publicly downloadable from guideherd.ai/images/social/ and listed in the existing root manifest.csv.
- Felt and paper-craft illustration, handmade texture, soft even light, cream background, muted blue, coral red, mustard yellow and sage green, calm uncluttered object scenes with empty space.
- No text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, brand marks, robots, faces on machines, screens, UI, fake product screenshots, distorted hands or faces, or generic off-topic imagery.
- Visual metaphors: folders, threads, paths, desks, trays, envelopes, phone, clipboard. People stay in charge.
- Claude downloads and visually inspects every asset before publishing; rejects invalid images.

## Creative diversity and budget
- Three weekly editorial themes, unique platform-specific creative executions where useful. Never force the same image onto all four platforms.
- The previous 12–14 visual asset estimate is a long-term creative capacity target, **not** a requirement to generate 12–14 paid images each week. Actual run rules require approximately 9–11 image placements weekly, depending on carousel and text-only posts; choose a mix of distinct assets, not automatic duplicates.
- **Hard weekly Blotato generation cap: 275 credits** (the live brief says 50 credits used this week, 1370 balance on Oct 27). This replaces the earlier inaccurate 550-credit figure. Respect any tighter live cap, remaining balance and plan allowance. No auto-purchases or plan upgrades.
- Prefer ChatGPT-generated, approved, public media first *after Claude updates its playbook*, Blotato generation as a fallback, and existing approved unused library images when generation is unsuitable.
- Avoid recent concepts: Oct 26 three desks and coral thread; Oct 23 winding path and flags; Oct 21 filing basket and cabinet; Oct 14 clipboard tiles and stamp; Oct 12 vacation handoff.
- Wed Oct 28: how-we-build-it/shipped image and Instagram carousel; Fri Oct 30: Halloween already covered by library.

## Handoff
- Place PNG/JPG assets in root images/social/, append filename/category/theme/format to root manifest.csv, verify live public URL and content before handing filenames to Claude.
- Claude owns publishing, live media checks and logging. ChatGPT owns image generation, asset QA and repository upload.
- No unapproved change to the schedule, credit purchases, product claims, or publishing privileges.

## Cross-industry positioning (owner direction)
- GuideHerd is positioned as general business automation across industries, not as a law-firm-only solution. Current law-firm deployments are examples, not the brand boundary.
- Core message: **The work between conversations** — scheduling, follow-ups, documents, records, routing, and handoffs after calls, meetings, and requests.
- Creative themes: invisible workload; people remain in charge; practical connected operations.
- Default to universal office-object imagery and cross-industry scenarios. Do not use gavels, courthouses, scales of justice, or legal-exclusive cues in general-audience creative.
- Illustrative scenarios in other verticals are hypothetical unless publicly verified. Do not imply customers, deployments, certifications, integrations, regulated-data compliance, or capabilities that have not been established.
- Preserve platform-specific copy and visual variation; test performance against the cross-industry positioning.

## Bold campaign: platform-native execution (owner direction, Oct 2026)
- New creative direction: bold concise headlines, polished dimensional illustrations, strong contrast, clever business-process metaphors, inspired by effective productivity-company social design without copying branding or artwork. Industry-neutral: the work between conversations.
- Each platform must have a **distinct creative treatment**; do not merely resize the same image and repost the same caption. Prepare separate platform-specific briefs and assets, with native copy, hooks, crops/compositions, and calls to conversation.
- **X:** short pointed observation, under 280 characters, punchy and provocative; visual on Monday and Friday, Wednesday text-only under current posting rule; no hashtags or links.
- **Threads:** warm, conversational, witty or reflective 200–450 characters; invite discussion; different visual composition from X; 0–1 hashtag.
- **Facebook:** practical owner/manager story or lesson, 100–180 words, one image, no hashtags; links only in first comment.
- **Instagram:** most visual/polished version; bold typography/strong visual storytelling, 3–6 short caption lines, weekly 2–3 panel carousel, no links and at most 3 hashtags.
- Bold editorial typography is **intentionally allowed** in this new approved creative family. Previous blanket no-text restriction applies only to felt/paper-craft image-generation style, not approved editorial graphics. Check typography legibility and claims before publishing.
- Four initial concepts: “The call ended. The work didn't.”, “Who owns the next step?”, “Less chasing. More doing.”, “The work between conversations.” These are **concepts, not permission to publish unreviewed assets**.
- Publishing safety: use only public, tested image URLs listed in root images/social/manifest.csv. No implied integrations, client results, unsupported claims or fabricated metrics. Maintain existing 275-credit weekly cap and fallback behavior. Never route social work to engineering wood chipper or Jarvis.
- Track per-platform impressions/views, engagement, comments, and creative variant. Compare to each platform's own baseline; do not assume results from another platform transfer.
