How a new case comes in

From the first ring to an opened matter, with a person in charge at every step.

  1. 1 Reception Guide

    The call is answered

    Your receptionist answers. GuideHerd's reception console helps them note who's calling and why, start the client's intake while they're on the line, and pass the call to scheduling with those details filled in.

  2. 2 Scheduling Guide

    Booked before they hang up

    Scheduling picks up with the caller's details already filled in. It offers real times from the right attorney's calendar and books the consultation, so the caller leaves with a confirmed appointment.

  3. 3 Intake Guide

    The client does the intake

    Before the meeting, the client fills in the intake on their phone and uploads files and photos.

  4. 4 Your staff approve

    The matter opens

    Staff review and approve. The client and matter are set up in your practice-management system, or staged for your staff to enter, and the documents are filed to your file server or Google Drive.
