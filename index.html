Why firms can do this now.

Nothing about the work has changed. What changed is that the calendar, the mailbox, and the appointment can now sit in one connected environment instead of three. GuideHerd connects to Microsoft 365, so for a firm already running there it reads real availability from the attorneys’ own calendars, creates the appointment on those calendars, and sends the confirmation from the firm’s own mailbox. There is no parallel calendar for the office to keep in sync, and nothing for your team to copy across.

Every conversation leaves work behind.

Somebody wrote the caller’s details on a pad. Somebody else checked whether the attorney was free, typed the appointment into a calendar, sent a confirmation, and made a note to follow up. None of that is legal work. All of it has to happen, in order, every time.

It is also where things quietly go wrong — the double-booking, the confirmation that never went out, the caller who was told someone would ring back. The cost is not one dramatic failure. It is the steady tax of coordination, paid by whoever happens to be at the desk.

GuideHerd takes that work as its job. One conversation ends; the platform records what was said, offers real times from the right attorney’s calendar, books it, confirms it, gives the client a way to change it, and keeps a record of all of it. Your team stays in charge and stays informed — they simply stop being the integration between one step and the next.
