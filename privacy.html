Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 August 2026

This policy describes what GuideHerd collects today, why, and who else is involved. GuideHerd has three separate surfaces and they do not work the same way, so this policy takes them one at a time.

Who we are

GuideHerd LLC (“GuideHerd”, “we”, “us”) builds software that turns conversations into completed work. Write to us at hello@guideherd.ai with any question about this policy or about information we hold.

What we collect depends entirely on which part of GuideHerd you are using.

This website

These marketing pages set no cookies and write nothing to your browser’s storage. There is no login and no account to create.

The contact form on our Let’s talk page does not send anything to us directly. It assembles a message and hands it to your own email application, and nothing leaves your computer until you choose to send it. Until you press send in your own mail client, we have received nothing at all. When you do send it, we receive the email you wrote and reply to it, and that message then lives in our business email like any other correspondence.

Two third parties are involved in serving this website, and your browser contacts them when a page loads:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and provides its traffic measurement. Cloudflare receives the network information that any web request carries, including your IP address, and reports aggregate figures to us. We do not build visitor profiles and we do not run advertising trackers.
  • unpkg, a public delivery network for open-source software, serves JavaScript libraries used by this site’s interface. Requesting them exposes your IP address to that network, in the same way that loading any external resource does.

Our service status page additionally fetches a published status file from GitHub, deliberately, so that it keeps working when the rest of the platform does not.

GuideHerd Academy

The Academy at training.guideherd.ai is a self-serve training subscription. When you subscribe, we store in our own database:

  • your email address and the name you give at checkout;
  • your subscription state — which plan, whether it is active, when the current period ends, and whether it is set to renew;
  • identifiers that connect your account to your payment record;
  • an audit history of account events, which records what happened and when so an access decision can be explained afterwards.

Payment

We never see or store your card details. Payment is handled entirely by Stripe. You enter your card on Stripe’s own checkout page, Stripe processes the payment, and we receive back only the identifiers we need in order to know that your subscription exists and is current. Stripe’s handling of your payment information is governed by Stripe’s own terms and privacy policy. When you manage or cancel a subscription, you do so in a billing page Stripe hosts.

Staying signed in

Signing in to the Academy sets one cookie, gh_session. It holds a signed reference to your account — not your password, and no personal information. It is marked HttpOnly and Secure, is restricted to our own site, and expires after 30 days. Its only purpose is to keep you signed in. It is not used for advertising and not used to follow you across other sites.

Information we hold for an organization

The GuideHerd platform is used by organizations that contract with us. When a firm uses GuideHerd, the information moving through it — the people who contacted that firm, what they said, what was scheduled, what was recorded, what was filed — belongs to that organization, not to us. We hold and process it on that organization’s instructions, for the purpose of running the service it asked us to run. We do not use it to market to anyone, and we do not use it for our own purposes.

If you are a client or a caller of an organization that uses GuideHerd and you want to know what is held about you, or want it corrected or deleted, please contact that organization directly. They control it and the decision is theirs. We support them in answering you, including with tooling to export and erase the records tied to one person, but we act on their instruction rather than in place of them.

Signing in to the platform

Staff sign in either through Google Workspace or with an account their own organization issues, using an email address and a password, optionally with a second factor from an authenticator application. Where a federated provider is used, that provider tells us who you are; we never receive or store your password for it.

How we protect information

What follows describes our practices. It is not a guarantee, and we would rather say that plainly than imply otherwise.

  • Traffic between you and GuideHerd is encrypted in transit.
  • Credentials are never displayed in our interfaces and never written into our logs.
  • Each organization’s data is separated from every other organization’s by construction, and that separation is covered by tests that run on every change.
  • Configuration is versioned and auditable, so a change can be traced, explained, and reversed.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the people who need it, and is reviewed.

No system is beyond every risk, and we do not claim to be. No outside party has assessed our security posture. If that changes, we will say so directly and name who did it, rather than implying it.

How long we keep information

Academy account records are kept while your account exists, and for a reasonable period afterwards for financial and record-keeping purposes. Information we hold for an organization is retained according to that organization’s instructions and the agreement we hold with it. If you want to know what we hold about you, ask us at hello@guideherd.ai and we will tell you.

Your choices

  • Your Academy account: write to us and we will correct or delete your account information.
  • Email from us: we email you in reply to something you sent, or about a subscription you hold. We do not add you to a marketing list you did not ask for.
  • Information held for an organization: contact that organization — they decide, and we support them.

Depending on where you live, local law may give you further rights over information about you. Write to us and we will work out how to honour them.

Children

GuideHerd is built for professional and business use. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

Where a change materially affects people who already hold an account with us, we will tell them rather than relying on this page to notice on their behalf.
